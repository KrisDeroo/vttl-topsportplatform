Consent for processing of operational data — version 1.0.0

VTTL Topsport (Vlaamse Tafeltennis Liga, Flemish Table Tennis League) processes personal data of players, coaches, parents, sparring partners and academy managers for the operational running of the elite-sports platform. This text describes which data we process, why, how long and which rights you have.

  • Which data? Surname, first name, date of birth, email address, phone number, address, club, academy, status (A/B/C), age category, preferred language, role on the platform.
  • Purpose: managing the player roster, training registration, calendar coordination, internal communication, evaluations and ranking follow-up.
  • Retention period: as long as the account is active, plus 5 years after deactivation for administrative and legal obligations. Automatic anonymisation thereafter.
  • Legal basis: GDPR Art. 6(1)(b) — performance of the elite-sports support contract between you and VTTL.
  • Recipients: limited to the Technical Director, certified coaches and academy managers with a functional need. No transfer to third parties outside the European Economic Area.
  • Rights: access, rectification, erasure, restriction, objection and portability via /my-data or dpo@vttl.be. You may also lodge a complaint with the Belgian Data Protection Authority (GBA).
