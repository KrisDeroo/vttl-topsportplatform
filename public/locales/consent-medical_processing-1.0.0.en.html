Consent for processing of medical data — version 1.0.0

VTTL Topsport requests your explicit consent to process medical data in the context of sports-medical follow-up. Medical data is a special category of personal data under the GDPR and is technically isolated in a dedicated table with encrypted diagnosis fields, accessible only to medically mandated personnel.

  • Which data? Injury history, physical assessments, diagnosis codes, treatment plans, medical imaging (MRI/X-ray), medication information, return-to-play protocols.
  • Purpose: sports-medical follow-up, injury prevention, return-to-competition, and sharing of anonymised aggregated data with medical staff for care-quality purposes.
  • Retention period: 30 years in accordance with the Belgian Patient Rights Act. Subsequently archived in encrypted cold storage.
  • Legal basis: GDPR Art. 9(2)(h) — preventive medicine and sports-medical assessment, with explicit consent (Art. 9(2)(a)) as additional basis.
  • Access: exclusively medical staff and the Technical Director (the latter only for administrative handling, not content). Every read action is irreversibly logged in the audit log.
  • Withdrawal: this consent can be withdrawn at any time via /my-data or dpo@vttl.be. Withdrawal blocks future processing; data already recorded is anonymised or, where legally required, retained until the end of the retention period.
  • Optional: this consent is not required to participate in trainings or competitions, but without it the medical staff cannot support you with injuries or return-to-play.
