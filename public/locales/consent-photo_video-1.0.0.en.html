Consent for use of photo and video material — version 1.0.0

VTTL Topsport requests your consent to capture and use photo and video material of trainings, matches and training camps. Footage may be used for sports-technical analysis, follow-up of playing technique, internal communication between coaches, and — only with separate explicit confirmation — for external communication via VTTL channels.

  • Which material? Photos and video clips in which you are identifiable, captured during trainings, sparring sessions, matches and camps organised by VTTL or a linked academy.
  • Purpose:
    • Sports-technical analysis (stroke build-up, footwork, tactical decisions) — internal use between you and your coach;
    • Storage in your player dossier for longitudinal follow-up;
    • Optional (separate consent): external communication on the VTTL website / social media / newsletters.
  • Retention period: as long as the account is active. Upon account deactivation: clips no longer relevant to ongoing analysis are deleted within 12 months; analysis clips that form a historical part of your development dossier remain in encrypted storage for up to 5 years after deactivation.
  • Legal basis: GDPR Art. 6(1)(a) — consent. For minors under 16, additional parental consent is required (Art. 8 GDPR + Belgian implementation).
  • Access: your own coach, the Technical Director, and (for external communication) the VTTL communication officer. No access for other players or third parties without explicit additional consent.
  • Withdrawal: this consent can be withdrawn at any time via /my-data or dpo@vttl.be. Upon withdrawal, no new material is captured; existing material is anonymised (face rendered unrecognisable) or deleted within 30 days. Material already published externally is actively retracted.
  • No consequences: withdrawal has no impact on your sporting participation, training opportunities or status within VTTL.
